Campus emergencies move faster than campus coordination.

When a student urgently needs blood or medical assistance, the people who could help — a compatible donor two buildings away, a coordinator with access to campus medical resources — often aren't found in time. LifelineX exists to close that gap, using only information students have explicitly chosen to share.

The response sequence

From request to resolution

  1. 01

    Request is created

    A student or campus medical coordinator opens LifelineX and submits an emergency request — blood or a medical resource — with urgency and location.

  2. 02

    Responders are matched

    The system searches opted-in responders nearby first, expanding the radius in stages — 500m, 1km, 3km — only if needed.

  3. 03

    Alerts go out

    Matched responders receive a high-priority, clearly labeled emergency alert with distance, urgency, and what's needed — nothing more.

  4. 04

    Response is confirmed

    A responder accepts, the coordinator dashboard updates in real time, and further alerts stop once enough responders are found.

Built on consent

Every signal is opt-in, every view is minimal

◎

Opt-in, always

Location sharing and emergency alerts are off by default. Students choose to become responders, and can turn it off any time.

▤

Minimum necessary detail

Responders see approximate distance and urgency — never a student's exact location or private medical history.

◇

Deterministic matching

Responders are ranked by resource match, distance, and availability using clear rules — not an AI making medical decisions.

▣

Coordinator oversight

An admin dashboard gives campus medical coordinators full visibility into active emergencies and responder status.

Prototype disclosure

LifelineX is a hackathon prototype, not a medical system.

It does not diagnose, verify blood compatibility, or authorize donations or medication distribution. All blood group and medical information is self-reported and must be verified by qualified medical staff before any real-world donation, transfusion, or medicine distribution. LifelineX only coordinates communication between opted-in students and authorized campus medical resources.
